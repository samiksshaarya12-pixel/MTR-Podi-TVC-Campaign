# MTR-Podi-TVC-Campaign
Account Manager for MTR Podi Powder’s regional TVC campaign, coordinating between the brand and production house to deliver Telugu and Kannada adaptations celebrating South Indian culinary traditions, regional flavours, and MTR’s authentic taste.

## Project Overview
A regional advertising campaign for MTR's Podi Powder range, featuring two 30-second TVC adaptations for Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka.

## Creative Concept
The campaign celebrates South Indian culinary traditions through the story of a newly married woman seeking the approval of three family matriarchs. By serving MTR podi powders with traditional dishes, she wins their approval, connecting the brand with the timeless flavours of South Indian homes.

## My Role: Account Manager
I managed coordination between the brand and the production house, helping align campaign requirements with creative production deliverables.

My responsibilities included:
- Brand and production-house coordination
- Stakeholder communication and alignment
- Coordination of regional TVC adaptations

## Deliverables
- Telugu TVC for Andhra Pradesh
- Kannada TVC for Karnataka
- Two 30-second TVC scripts
